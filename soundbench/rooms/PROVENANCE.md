# Recording-chain parameters

`philharmonia-banjo-chain.json` is a numerical recording-chain estimate fitted to Philharmonia Orchestra isolated banjo notes.
It contains room/microphone/background/codec model parameters, not a sampled impulse response or audio excerpt.

Source: [Philharmonia sound samples](https://philharmonia.co.uk/resources/sound-samples/), Strings archive.
The source page permits musical use, including commercial works, but restricts making samples available as-is or as a sampler instrument.
That is custom permission, not CC0.
The instrument source assignments and inferred recording chain do not establish the microphone, room or banjo construction as historical facts.

The parameters are MIT-licensed with the rest of the library; the source's terms cover its samples, none of which are included.

`nagra.toml` sets the tape machine, microphones and room as physical and design priors checked against recording diagnostics; no impulse response or reference waveform is bundled.
